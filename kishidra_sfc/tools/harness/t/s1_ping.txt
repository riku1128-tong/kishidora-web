# S1: an XDEFS=TEST_HOOKS ROM answers DC_PING through the sys.c mailbox (dbg_res[0] = ~dbg_arg[0]),
# and DC_UNKNOWN (ack $ff) for a command no one knows.  (A ROM without TEST_HOOKS has no dbg_* symbols:
# runrom stops with exit status 2 at the first poke; abtest.py skips the script there.)
#!needs dbg_cmd
wait 300
pokew dbg_arg $1234
poke dbg_cmd 1
waitfor dbg_seq 1 10
peek dbg_ack
peek dbg_res 6
wram OUT/ping.bin dbg_cmd 20
poke dbg_cmd $42
waitfor dbg_seq 2 10
peek dbg_ack
wram OUT/unknown.bin dbg_cmd 20
